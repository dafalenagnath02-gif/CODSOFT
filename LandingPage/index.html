About GADKOT VATSARU

GADKOT VATSARU is a trekking and travelling community dedicated to exploring forts, preserving Swarajya heritage and promoting Marathi culture.

We organize treks, historical visits and awareness activities to help people connect with the rich history of Maharashtra.
